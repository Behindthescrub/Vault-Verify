## 3. Selling points (feature → benefit → proof)

### Skills Checklist (VaultVerify document)
- **Honest by design** — every checklist is labeled "Self-assessed — not
  independently verified". No inflated third-party claims, no fake references.
  *Proof: the attestation block the candidate signs electronically.*
- **Clinically accurate templates** — 315 ready checklists / 32,153 skills
  covering RN, LPN/CNA, PT, OT, RRT and allied professions (Leadership, ICU,
  Med-Surg, ER, L&D…). Imported from the MyZipVault skills model.
  *Proof: "Nursing | Healthcare Leadership | Leadership — 38 skills" sample.*
- **Per-skill honesty signals** — 1–4 proficiency scale with N/A, PLUS "last
  performed" recency (within 3/6 months, 6+ months ago). A "4" from 2 years ago
  is not the same as a "4" from last month — we surface that instantly.
- **Summary at a glance** — average rating, rating mix donut (Proficient /
  Experienced / Limited / No theory), recency donut, category-level averages.
  A recruiter judges a candidate in 10 seconds.
- **30-day validity** — every completed checklist is stamped "Valid until …"
  and expires automatically. Fresh data only.
- **Candidate attestation + e-signature** — "I confirm the ratings are my own
  and reflect my real experience." Protects the agency, deters inflation.
- **One-link candidate flow** — recruiter sends the link (email via Brevo or
  copy), candidate fills it on their phone in ~10 minutes, no account needed.
  Invitation emails arrive from **VaultVerify** (myzipvault@gmail.com) —
  the brand is on every message the candidate sees, not a faceless no-reply.
- **Six branded email templates, admin-managed** — invitation, reminder,
  submission confirmation (candidate), results notification (recruiter),
  welcome (new recruiter), platform test. Every email the platform sends is
  VaultVerify-branded (dark-teal header, green CTA, mint footer) and editable
  in Superadmin → Email with a live desktop/mobile preview, per-template test
  send and one-click reset to default. *Proof: the superadmin live preview
  renders exactly what a test send delivers.*
- **Beautiful branded PDF** — the VaultVerify-styled document (teal band,
  donuts, rating badges, per-category breakdown) with "Generated by …" stamp.
  The output looks like a professional credential, not a spreadsheet printout.
- **Manual-entry fallback** — recruiter can enter answers from a phone call or
  paper copy; the document is labeled "Entered by recruiter".

### Bill-to-Pay Staffing Calculator
- **Margin math that survives audit** — bill rate → pay rate with overtime,
  onboarding %, worker's comp, GSA per-diem lookup, traveler stipend splits.
- **Every run saved & reloadable** — compare offers side by side.

### Platform (superadmin)
- **Your data, your platform** — skills data import/export in xlsx, Brevo email
  (verified live: real sends from the VaultVerify sender), user management,
  active-session control.
- **Security basics done right** — bcrypt-hashed passwords, server-side
  sessions, public candidate links are single-purpose tokens. Platform keys
  (Brevo, GSA per-diem) are set ONLY by the superadmin — recruiter accounts
  never see or override them; the GSA key stays server-side in every request.
